Questions? We have answers

Frequently asked questions

Is EasyBuy really free?

Yes. EasyBuy is completely free for Pakistani sellers. No monthly fees, no commissions on Cash on Delivery orders, no setup charges. We may add optional paid features in the future, but the core platform will remain free.

How is this different from Shopify?

Shopify costs around Rs. 11,000 per month and is built for global businesses. EasyBuy is built specifically for Pakistan — free, Cash on Delivery first, WhatsApp native, with Easypaisa and JazzCash built in.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. You only need an email address. Sign up, create your store, and start taking orders within minutes.

Can I use my own custom domain?

Every EasyBuy store gets a free easybuy.pk link. Custom domains will be available soon.

How do I get paid?

With Cash on Delivery, you keep 100% of the money the courier collects — nothing comes to EasyBuy. You can also accept Bank, Easypaisa and JazzCash payments, which go directly to your account.

Can I sell internationally?

Yes. Add any of 21 supported markets, and international customers see prices in their own currency. You keep receiving payments your way.
